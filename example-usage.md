# Example Usage

Here's an example of how to use the Company Interview Preparation Research Agent:

## Sample Input

When you run `npm start`, you'll be prompted with the following questions:

```
Enter the company name: Google
Enter the job description: We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer to join our Cloud Infrastructure team. You will work on designing and implementing scalable distributed systems, optimizing performance, and collaborating with cross-functional teams to deliver high-quality software solutions.
Enter the role/position title: Senior Software Engineer - Cloud Infrastructure
Enter research breadth (recommended 4-8, default 6): 6
Enter research depth (recommended 2-4, default 3): 3
```

## Follow-up Questions

The system will then ask personalized follow-up questions like:

- What is your current experience level with cloud technologies and distributed systems?
- Do you have any specific concerns about Google's interview process or technical requirements?
- Are you more interested in focusing on system design, coding interviews, or behavioral questions?
- What timeline are you working with for your interview preparation?

## Generated Report

The system will generate a comprehensive report saved as `google_interview_prep_report.md` containing:

### 1. Company Overview
- Google's mission, values, and culture
- Recent developments and company news
- Work environment and benefits
- Industry position and competitors

### 2. Role Analysis
- Specific technical requirements for Cloud Infrastructure roles
- Required programming languages and frameworks
- System design and architecture knowledge
- Leadership and collaboration skills

### 3. Interview Process
- Phone screening and technical phone interviews
- On-site interviews: coding, system design, behavioral
- Timeline: typically 2-4 weeks
- Interview format and preparation tips

### 4. Preparation Strategy
- Technical preparation: algorithms, data structures, system design
- Behavioral preparation using STAR method
- Google-specific preparation: company culture, recent projects
- Mock interview suggestions and practice resources

### 5. Common Interview Questions
- Technical: "Design a distributed cache system"
- Behavioral: "Tell me about a time you optimized system performance"
- Company-specific: "How would you scale Google Search?"
- Questions to ask: "What are the biggest technical challenges your team faces?"

### 6. Success Tips and Best Practices
- Interview day preparation checklist
- Communication strategies for technical discussions
- Follow-up actions and thank you notes
- Red flags to watch for during the process

## Output File

The final report will be saved as: `google_interview_prep_report.md`

This comprehensive report provides everything you need to prepare effectively for your interview at Google!
